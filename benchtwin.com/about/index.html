Build2Real: build it before you buy it

Build2Real is a free web app for designing, wiring, coding and simulating electronics and robots on a 3D bench, right in your browser. Prototype without parts: try a circuit or a robot before you buy anything, and see what goes wrong without breaking a thing.

Build2Real is free to use.

Open Build2Real

Who made Build2Real

Build2Real was founded by Mohit Nagar. This application helps people to work on their project. They built it so anyone can try a circuit or a robot before they have the parts, without the risk of burning out a board.

Build2Real is made for students, makers and robotics teams: people learning electronics for the first time, classes without a full lab, and teams who want to test a robot before they build it.

What Build2Real is

Build2Real is a microcontroller and robotics simulator that runs in your web browser, with nothing to install. You put real parts on a 3D bench, wire them pin to pin, write the code, and press Simulate to watch it work, the way it would on your desk. The parts behave like the real ones: if something wouldn’t work in real life, it doesn’t work in Build2Real either, and it tells you why in plain words.

What you can do

  • Build: put real parts (Arduino, ESP32, sensors, motors, screens and LEGO SPIKE, VEX, FTC and FRC robot kits) on a 3D bench, each the size of the real thing.
  • Wire: connect them pin to pin. Circuit checks explain mistakes in plain words, before anything burns.
  • Code: write Arduino and ESP32 sketches, SPIKE and VEXcode Python, or FTC and FRC Java.
  • Simulate: LEDs light, motors turn, screens draw and the serial monitor talks back.
  • Design: sketch your own parts in 3D and export them for 3D printing.
  • Test Bench: drive your robot on a competition field with real physics.

Accounts and signing in

You can use Build2Real without an account. With a free account you can save projects to the cloud and open them on any device. You can sign up with your email address, or log in with Google, GitHub or Microsoft. When you log in with Google, Build2Real receives only your name, email address and profile picture, and uses them only to create your account, log you in and greet you by name. It doesn’t access anything else in your Google account.
