Drag to turn the bench. Click a part to look closer.

Build it before you buy it.

Put real parts on a 3D bench, wire them pin to pin, write the code and watch it run. Design your own parts, then drive your robot on a competition field, right in your browser.

Runs code on 159 boards, and LEGO SPIKE, VEX, FTC and FRC robot programs.

Start from a project

Open a ready-made project, see how it’s wired and make it your own.

Made for makers, robotics teams and classrooms

Real electronics and real robot code, without waiting for parts or worrying about breaking them.

Mistakes cost nothing

Wire it wrong, short a battery or burn an LED with Safety mode off. Nothing breaks for real, and the checks explain what happened.

Real parts, real sizes

Boards, sensors and robot kits from real catalogs, with their pins where the real ones are, so what you build is what you buy.

Your team’s real code

SPIKE and VEXcode Python, FTC and FRC Java: press Run and watch your autonomous drive on its real field, with encoders, sensors and the real time limit.

Lab tools

A multimeter, an oscilloscope and a pin timeline measure the circuit while it runs.

Ready for the classroom

Nothing to install: it runs in the browser, on Chromebooks too. Lessons check every step, in six languages.

Exports engineers use

A KiCad board and netlist, a parts list to buy from, STL files for printing and the Arduino code.

Learn Build2Real

A free course, from your first LED to a robot on its field. Every lesson has a mini Build2Real window where you wire, build and code it yourself.

    About us

    Build2Real was founded by Mohit Nagar. This application helps people to work on their project.

    We made it so anyone can try a circuit or a robot before they have the parts, without the risk of burning out a board. It’s for students, makers and robotics teams, and it’s free.
